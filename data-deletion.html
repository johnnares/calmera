Calmera – Data Deletion

Calmera does not require or provide user accounts. User data is stored locally on the user's device and is not stored on Calmera servers.

How to delete your data

You can delete your locally stored rating history directly from the Calmera app:

Settings → Your data → Delete rating history

Uninstalling the Calmera app also removes the locally stored application data from the device, subject to the device's operating system behavior and backup settings.
