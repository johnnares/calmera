Calmera: Relax & Sleep - Privacy Policy

Developer: Soulera Productions · Effective date: 7 October 2026

In short: Calmera has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What it learns about your preferences is stored only on your phone. The only information that leaves your phone is an approximate position, and only when you switch on the features that need it (described below).

1. Information stored only on your phone

  • Your settings: which sounds are on, their density and volume, and which features you enabled.
  • Your ratings (like / okay / dislike). With each rating the app stores the sound and its settings and, only for the personalisation features you enabled, the day of the week and time of day, an approximate area (a grid square of about 1 km, never your exact position), the weather type (for example "cloudy") and your movement type (for example "walking"). Ratings older than 90 days are deleted automatically.
  • A small movement diagnostics file (step rhythm, speed and the detected movement type, with no position), used to check the movement detection. Its size is limited.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is not included in Android cloud backups. You can delete all ratings at any time in Settings > Your data > Delete rating history, and uninstalling the app deletes everything.

We do not sell, rent or share your information for advertising. We do not run any servers that receive your data.

3. Permissions

  • Location: only asked for, and only used, when you switch on the Location, Weather or Movement speed features. Location is used while the app is open or while it is playing sound (with its notification visible); the app does not use location in the background otherwise.
  • Physical activity: used to read the phone's step counter to tell walking, jogging and running apart (Movement speed feature). The step rhythm stays on your phone and is never sent.
  • Notifications and foreground service: show the playback controls and keep the sound playing while the screen is off.
  • Internet: only for the weather requests described above.

Every feature can be switched off in Settings, and Android lets you withdraw any permission at any time.

4. Children

Calmera is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
